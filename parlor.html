The Parlor · games for two

Draw a card. Both answer.

Two chairs by the fire, no phones, no winners. Pick your decks below, draw a card, and take turns answering out loud. The deck remembers what you’ve drawn, so every card is new until the deck runs dry.

House rules: both of you answer every card, asker goes second. No wrong answers, no using an answer against someone later. Pass on any card, no explanation owed. One card can last five minutes or the whole evening — there is no clock in here.
