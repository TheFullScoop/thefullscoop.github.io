The full picture. A better way to be together.

The Full Scoop has two jobs: help you understand what is happening, and make room for people to understand each other.

The Library: a source of truth you can inspect

The Library keeps one living book for each subject. New developments become dated chapters, so the record grows instead of starting over. Every factual claim points to its source. When a question is contested, the book gives the strongest sourced case on both sides, with no verdict attached. You see the evidence and decide for yourself. See the Library.

The Parlor: a place for human connection

The rest of the site is for people to use with each other. The Parlor begins with question decks for two: both people answer, either may pass, and there are no winners. Over time, this side of the house can grow more ways to read together, talk honestly, write to someone, and gather in person. The site builds the room; people make the connection. Visit the Parlor.

How the house works

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. No feed to keep up with. The Library closes on Sundays; the Parlor stays open. Anything the Parlor remembers stays on your device. These rules are part of the point: the site should help with the thing you came for, then leave you free to go live your life. Compare the current About page.
