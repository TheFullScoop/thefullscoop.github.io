## 2. Longevity & Healthspan — `longevity.html`  →  Chapter 3
**"The GLP-1 pill reaches the counter"**

Rationale: Ch.1 predicted oral orforglipron would "widen access dramatically." This week it
actually did — the honest, non-hype angle is *access arriving, evidence unchanged.*

- **The pill hit the pharmacy.** Orforglipron (now Foundayo) — first GLP-1 pill, no food/water/
  timing restrictions, ~14–15% weight loss at 72 wks — became reachable at ~$50/mo for eligible
  Medicare Part D patients starting July 1.
- **Access ≠ evidence.** It's approved for obesity, not aging; the longevity case still rests on
  the injectable mortality/CV data plus the one preliminary aging-clock signal from Ch.2.
- **A population-scale experiment.** Cheaper/easier means far more exposure before the longevity
  question is settled. Two-sided block: *is cheap, easy access good for healthspan?* (public-
  health win vs. ahead of the evidence).
